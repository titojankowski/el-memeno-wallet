What "no copyrighted IP" means

Every Meme Card is released under CC0, a public domain license. The artist gives up their copyright, so anyone can use, remix or sell the art without asking (6529's license page).

You can only give away rights you own. So leave out other people's characters, logos, brand names, photos, song lyrics and movie stills. Your own drawings, your own photos, public domain images and other CC0 art, including existing Meme Cards, are all fine.
